# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Being kind and assuming good faith
- Accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its players
- Showing patience with people whose first language is not yours. This is an
  Arabic-language project with an international codebase, and both directions of
  that gap are normal

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, or personal and political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Deliberately spoiling answers for other players

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and will take fair
corrective action in response to behaviour they deem inappropriate, including
editing or removing contributions, and temporary or permanent bans.

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour by opening a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/alkhatibdev/arabic-semantic-game/security/advisories/new),
which is the most private channel this repository offers. All complaints will be
reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy will
be respected.
